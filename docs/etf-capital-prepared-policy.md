# Shared policy from privately owned preparation

The public `capital_daily_policy` remains the complete original-projection
entry point. Its historical v1 policy hashes and output behavior are unchanged.
Its final instruction kernel is shared with the private owning-evaluator path,
not duplicated. Neither path approves an account or issues a broker order.

Private preparation v2 adds explicit200-bar history readiness, per-symbol
close-at-or-below-SMA200 facts, canonical config identity and the original
declared session sequence. Before warmup, SMA facts are unknown, not false.
These facts share the already validated full original projection/basis hashes;
no public signal or projection schema is changed. V1 private preparation
identities are distinct historical calculations, never silently relabeled v2.

The private consumer selects the original opening candidate's signal, preserves
its symbol and stop distance, and counts holding sessions by the original
calendar ordinal even on a sparse preparation schedule. A new fold candidate
cannot replace that opening policy. Known maximum hold exits precede feature
warmup. Momentum eligibility, mean-reversion RSI/SMA invalidation and rotation
winner changes use the same shared instruction kernel and canonical regime-exit
configuration as the public path. Unknown held SMA information denies; it is
never interpreted as permission to hold. Zero/unknown ATR cannot create entry.

This boundary is private and invocation-local. Constructing a private dataclass
does not authenticate a source, an execution route, opening ownership, cost or
risk approval. The implemented original-event owner/panel reconstructs these facts from its
own original request and bind unique BUY/fill events before using an opening
policy. There is no public prepared-token/economic-admission factory.

Source/cost/execution/economic/promotion flags remain false. Original scheduling,
train-derived ranking, account carryover and benchmark reports are composed in
the [panel](etf-capital-panel.md), not supplied by this helper alone. Current
release, full workload, executable freeze and qualified economics remain
incomplete gates. No study, brokerage call, trade,
deployment or production risk change is implied by this software.
