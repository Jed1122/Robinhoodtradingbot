# Invocation-owned capital risk progress

The offline daily owner can retain purpose-specific risk calculations only
inside its current invocation. Public risk/admission/recovery requests do not
accept progress objects, saved balances or saved latches. The ordinary batch
risk reader still reconstructs from genesis using the same point kernel.

Every call reconstructs the complete original account prefix and validates
canonical configuration. Continuation requires the same capital/config/action
mode, value-copied observation prefix and all original prefix economic hashes.
Changed or shortened inputs reconstruct from genesis; no previous account
balance is authoritative. All supplied observation fields and original
current/following-event time boundaries are checked, including old observations
against newly appended events. Action-observation coverage remains mandatory.

Only newly appended observations need their daily/weekly/drawdown calculations
repeated. Distinct purposes have separate derived points and loss decisions;
protective exits cannot inherit an entry-purpose permission or bypass a hard
stop. State is recorded only after the whole call validates. No progress is
persisted, imported, globally cached or promoted to source/account authority.

This is a workload optimization, not a changed risk policy, execution permission
or evidence of complete workload performance. Exact historical batch hashes,
full original-event accounting, UTC reset/gap-loss baselines, weekly/drawdown
latches, episode-completion clocks, reservations and settlement/fee obligations
remain in force. Research capital does not change production limits.
