# Descriptive stock portfolio metrics

`research.stock_monetary_metrics` summarizes a completed conditional retrospective
portfolio result. It uses the existing account, snapshots, expense marks, fills,
orders, lots and policy inventory. It submits no transaction and generates no
strategy, benchmark, recommendation or confidence estimate.

The public entry is `summarize_retrospective_monetary_result(result,
fresh_inputs=..., registration=...)`. It checks freshly supplied source inputs
through the existing finish boundary and binds the registered case/calendar and
definition identities. Freeze the registration before outcomes. Revision/tree
strings are controller-supplied bindings; a pure calculation does not authenticate
Git or the provider. `monetary_native_calendar_hash` binds the 706 native sessions
and excludes price-free calendar padding.

## Wealth and costs

- Gross equity G includes cash, long-term/tactical holdings, unsettled sale claims
  and earned unpaid dividend receivables. Paid fees and operating charges already
  affect G. Reservations and liquidity/tax reserves remain owned assets.
- Net equity N subtracts known due unpaid operating liabilities. Unknown due
  amounts make the required N curve unavailable.
- For separately simulated registered flat rates 0 or 25%, estimated
  pre-liquidation after-provision wealth is A=N-tax_reserve-rate*receivables.
  Rate None leaves A unavailable. These are explicit research scenarios, not
  the investor's bracket, paid taxes or final after-tax reporting. Unrealized
  share-gain liquidation tax is outside this estimate.

Missing/nonpositive observations retain their exact expected dates and counts;
the consumer does not obtain a valid curve by dropping them. Full/train/late
windows have 507/506, 239/238 and 269/268 marks/returns. The late period is reused.
Returns use fractional units, 252 trading-period annualization, zero risk-free
return, sample volatility and all-observation downside RMS. Undefined ratios and
unknown inherited peaks remain explicit. Budget ratios and scenario differences
are not automatically causal return drag.

## Activity and evidence coverage

Two-way turnover counts owned buy and sell notional, excluding fees, against the
full expected mean gross equity. Partial profit-taking rungs remain one owned
episode. Completed price-disposal P&L uses net proceeds minus disposed basis;
dividends, portfolio taxes and infrastructure are not assigned to episodes.
Ambiguous fill provenance makes affected activity metrics unavailable.

Final episode status can be known while historical order status/completion time
is unavailable: current experimental orders lack creation/cancellation clocks.
The last disposal determines market holding duration but does not necessarily
prove the time all active orders ceased. Ambiguous segment membership retains
an explicit reason and does not shrink the denominator.

Policy coverage is separate from portfolio returns. It retains all eight stocks,
4,056 audit slots and 4,048 executable-opportunity slots, with warmup/missing
evidence, known active/inactive signals and execution denials distinguished.
An unavailable case never becomes a successful starting-cash curve. All 56
constrained dependencies, eight quality allocator gaps, seven family labels and
declared missing comparison slots remain visible.

The report preserves the current replay limits: conditional omitted historical
screens, ordinary-close 75% SPY restoration, and incomplete quarterly rebalance,
volatility, corporate-action and long-term quality allocation controls. This
module supplies descriptive accounting, not a completed Task 5 study. Actual
source/scenario/benchmark registration, complete financial comparisons and the
statistical evaluation remain required. All qualification, investment, live and
full-completion flags remain false.
