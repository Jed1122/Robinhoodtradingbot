# Conditional stock monetary-input preparation

`research.stock_monetary_admission` prepares retained observations for a separately
defined retrospective accounting scenario. It executes no strategy, entitlement,
order or portfolio return. A prepared input graph is distinct from an eligible
investment or a completed economic study.

`StockMonetaryRequest` binds all nine ordered price slots, raw and split originals,
the native market calendar, two dated security-identity observations per symbol,
the entire original cash-distribution CSV and its derived views, and declared
receipt/hash/clock bindings. Preparation reparses whole native observations and
compares their derived fields exactly. It retains current receipt clocks and
native unknowns. The runtime must separately verify the actual envelope files;
declared hashes do not authenticate a provider.

The conditional profile names its retrospective USD/ticker correspondence and
observed-event inventory assumptions. Native daily quote currency remains
unknown. Endpoint agreement does not establish continuous security lineage or
complete corporate-action coverage. An unsupported price-unit conversion or a
conflicting graph produces typed unavailability; no source row is repaired or
silently removed to make a cohort pass.

Every result retains eight stock slots and a separate SPY benchmark, all seven
canonical strategy families, and all 56 stock/family dependency cases. Missing
risk, publication-aware financial/event, estimate-vintage, lifecycle, settlement,
funding or operating-cost dependencies remain explicit. A successful observation
preparation cannot turn those cases into profitable or validated cash strategies.
It never fabricates sector, market cap, spread, tradability or earnings clearance.

Events at or before the first possible purchase are classified as not earned by
the cash-start model. Later events are holding-dependent candidates; no entitlement
is asserted. Unpaid terminal candidates remain separate from cash. The module
generates no future market/settlement padding and grants no reinvestment funds.

The separate [replay schedule module](stock-replay-schedules.md) can compile
conditional market, settlement and dividend-funding tables, with calendar-only
padding. It retains its own source and assumption bindings; it does not change
this preparation result or make its unavailable constrained cases eligible.

Fictional tests and independent graph probes verify the boundaries. The separate
actual-input bridge, source-admitted monetary replay, complete strategy and cost
comparisons, and statistical report remain work to finish Task 5. All investment
and live-execution flags remain false.
