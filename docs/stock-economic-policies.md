# Seven-family research replay

`research.stock_economic_policies` connects the existing seven reproducible
strategy rules to the fictional account/session kernel. It evaluates alternative
hypotheses, without selecting a winner, recommending a stock or enabling trades.

The wrapper retains all eight declared stock slots and the separate SPY reference
in each of seven independent simulated accounts. It does not aggregate those
accounts as one portfolio. SPY supplies an explicitly labeled 75% passive
long-term baseline; it is excluded from tactical discovery and ranking. The
distinct stock-quality allocation remains unavailable until its complete source,
valuation, risk and selection contract exists.

At each close, `EconomicPolicyFrame` supplies causal observations. The kernel
binds their prices and calendar to its owned input rows and derives holdings from
actual modeled fills. A qualifying flat position enters; a qualifying held
position holds. Missing evidence remains a typed unavailable condition, blocks
new entry and does not become a family sell or a fabricated signal transition.
Known price-based safety and holding-time exits remain independent.

Each entry retains its family and first-fill age. Holding caps use the smaller
of 40 sessions and the existing family's maximum: mean reversion 5, breakout and
PEAD 20, revision momentum 30, and the remaining families 40. Partial fills cannot
reset age or profit-rung ownership. Final signals receive no invented execution
session. Restart and chronological continuation retain the same account history.

The absent optional seam preserves the original scripted-fixture path and its
evidence hashes. Defined/unavailable outcomes preserve all candidate and family
denominators. Native annual evidence uses validated bundle hashes, retaining
unused source provenance without treating those values as policy inputs.

Fictional tests and independent probes establish software behavior only. Actual
source admission, stock-quality allocation, complete rebalancing/volatility/cost
comparisons, statistical analysis and the full Task 5 study remain separate.
Research proposals have no broker, risk-authorization or live-execution authority.
