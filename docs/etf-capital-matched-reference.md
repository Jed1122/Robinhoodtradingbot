# Retrospective mean-exposure SPY and cash references

`research.etf_capital_matched.run_capital_matched_reference` owns an original
capital trajectory request and reconstructs it. Supplied balances, exposures,
cached preparations or result objects are not accepted. It averages the declared
test-window close gross held-notional/equity ratios. Distribution receivables
count once in equity; operating expenses do not shrink this denominator.
The baseline is the preceding original close; supplied later tail dates do not
enter the mean. The full comparison panel must separately bind its630 test dates.

This whole-window mean is retrospective and future-conditioned. It is not a
causal trading rule, attainable daily exposure replica, training score or
promotion evidence. Its mathematical initial SPY notional is the observed mean
times initial capital after the declared entry-fee reserve. Positive allocation
pays that fee once; a zero mean buys nothing and charges no fictional fees,
even when a declared purchase fee would be unaffordable. Original declared
costs remain in the matched identity; the existing full-passive purchase
affordability requirement is unchanged.
Ratios/mean use the existing64-digit context; funded notional is multiplied
exactly. Friction is the same approved round-trip whole-percent assumption,
split equally per side, embedded once. A liquidation proxy estimates the exit
fee and friction; no actual sale or final profit is manufactured.

The private shared passive valuation seam uses original SPY rows, initial-share
split units and ex-date cash entitlement. There is no DRIP or chained total-
return-factor reinvestment. Payments move receivables to cash without a second
gain. Unknown actions or unsupported coincident held split/distribution deny.
The existing public passive and descriptive path identities remain unchanged.

The cash comparison is the existing kernel's constant-capital zero-yield lower
bound, not measured customer interest. Expenses and taxes remain separately
reported assumptions/unknowns, not debits that authorize borrowing. All source,
cost, execution, economic-admission and promotion flags stay false. This adapter
does not establish the complete four-role statistical panel, qualified history,
customer execution calibration, workload feasibility or an economic edge.

Private composition now separates four original monetary terms from the public
single-candidate trajectory request. `_capital_matched_values` uses those terms
with an internally generated path; the existing public wrapper and matched
identity remain unchanged. A selected walk-forward path must not be disguised
as a public single-candidate request. This private seam is not saved-result
admission: its owning comparison must validate original source and window
frontiers before use.
